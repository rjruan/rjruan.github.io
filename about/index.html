About Ruby

I am an explorer who happens to carry a sketchbook, a prototype, and too many questions.

I'm Ruby—a curious explorer, UX designer, and emerging researcher. My path connects graphic design, philosophy, and web development with HCI, Human Factors, accessibility, XR, and human-centered AI.

Working Values

Curiosity, clarity, access, and agency.

I care about people, not abstract “users.” I want to understand what a system asks of someone, what it hides, and whether the person still has room to choose.

01

Observe People and Systems

Start by noticing context, behavior, friction, constraints, and the surrounding system before narrowing toward a solution.

02

Frame the Question

Translate messy situations into useful design and research questions, while keeping assumptions visible.

03

Prototype for Understanding

Use visual design, interaction design, and coded prototypes to test structure, clarity, accessibility, and agency.

04

Document What Remains Unknown

Treat open questions as part of the work, especially where evidence, permission, or deeper research is still needed.

Future Direction

Growing toward HCI, Human Factors, accessibility, XR, human-centered AI, and emerging human-centered technologies.

I am especially drawn to high-stakes and emerging systems where cognition, embodiment, accessibility, and human dignity meet. Graduate study is the next place I want to deepen the research methods behind those questions.
